Changes by Harmonic Atlas: selection of 107 stars, labels, brightness weights,
and north-up, east-left stereographic chart projections with uniform scaling.
Input coordinates are right ascension in hours and declination in degrees,
for epoch and equinox J2000. No runtime catalog download is required.
The adapted star data and derived coordinate fields remain CC BY-SA 4.0.
No endorsement by the catalog author is implied.

Source records, hash, and transformation script are included in the source
project: src/content/star-catalog.json, scripts/project-star-charts.mjs,
and the chart fields in src/content/editorial.json. These fields are separate
from the project's MIT code, original written content, and original figure art.
